01   Individual conditions

Break the requirement into atomic rules and clarify assumptions.

8 conditions · 2 assumptions
IDIndividual conditionRelevant values / interpretation
C1Promotional coupon countNone, one, or an attempted second coupon; maximum allowed is one.
C2Loyalty-points redemptionNone or one redemption on the same order as a coupon.
C3Coupon eligibility by item price statusRegular-price items are eligible; already-discounted sale items are ineligible for coupon discount.
C4Cart compositionRegular-price items only, sale items only, or a mixed cart.
C5Combined discount rateCombined coupon and points value is below, exactly at, or above 40% of order subtotal.
C6Order subtotalPositive subtotal; the cap uses order subtotal as its denominator.
C7Discount allocation and calculationCoupon must not discount sale items. The cart-level combined discount cap applies regardless of allocation.
C8State changesCart subtotal, coupon, or points amount may change; eligibility and cap must be recalculated.
A1Assumptions and unresolved behaviorTests use valid, otherwise eligible coupons and sufficient points unless stated. The requirement does not specify whether excess discounts are rejected or automatically reduced; either is acceptable only if the combined discount never exceeds 40%. Mixed-cart coupon behavior is tested as applying only to regular-price items; confirm if business rules instead reject the coupon for any cart containing a sale item.
A2Calculation precisionUse currency precision for assertions. Boundary cases should be repeated with the currency rounding rules supported by the application; the rounding method is not specified.
