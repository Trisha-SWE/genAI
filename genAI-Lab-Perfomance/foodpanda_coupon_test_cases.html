Foodpanda Coupon Feature — QA Test Cases

Functional, boundary, negative, usability, and security coverage | 43 independently executable cases

Scope note: The coupon behavior below is based on the rules supplied for this exercise. It is not a statement about verified Foodpanda implementation, actual coupon codes, or undocumented APIs. Ambiguous policy is marked for confirmation in the assumptions below.

Requirements and assumptions

  1. Requirements supplied for this test exercise (not verified product facts): Codes are unique; coupons can have minimum order values, percentage or fixed discounts, optional maximum discounts, validity windows, user/restaurant/item eligibility, per-customer or overall use limits, and configurable promotion combination. Checkout must calculate the discount accurately, honor rules, and never discount more than the eligible amount. These are assumed requirements only.
  2. Eligible subtotal and currency: Examples use ৳ (BDT) and whole taka. Unless a case says otherwise, coupon calculations use eligible food-item subtotal before delivery fees and taxes. Confirm actual currency precision, rounding mode, and whether minimum-order checks include/exclude fees, taxes, or other discounts.
  3. Minimum value and discount boundaries: Cases assume minimum is inclusive (subtotal equal to minimum qualifies), and maximum discount is an upper bound. CP-007 assumes a fixed discount above eligible subtotal is capped; confirm whether product policy caps it or rejects the coupon.
  4. Dates and time zones: Date/time test fixtures use an explicitly configured business timezone and instant. Confirm whether start/end are inclusive, how date-only expiry is interpreted, and which timezone governs validity.
  5. Input normalization: CP-017 assumes surrounding whitespace is trimmed; CP-018 assumes case-insensitive matching. Confirm case and whitespace policy and apply it consistently to uniqueness checks.
  6. Eligibility and stacking: CP-025 assumes only eligible line items form the discount base. CP-032/033 use fixture-defined combination rules and stacking order. Confirm mixed-cart behavior and calculation order for multiple promotions.
  7. Fees and tax: CP-034 assumes food-only coupon excludes delivery. CP-035 requires the configured tax rule; the exercise does not assert a jurisdiction-specific tax treatment. Confirm tax base and order of discount/tax computation.
  8. Usage consumption, concurrency, and cancellation: For concurrency, CP-030 assumes usage is atomically limited at order confirmation; confirm whether a coupon is reserved earlier and how reservations expire. CP-040 assumes eligible cancellations restore usage; define cancellation stage, refunds, and whether usage is restored.
  9. User-facing messages: Tests assert that a clear, appropriate reason is provided, but exact message copy/localization is not specified. Define approved messages for invalid, expired, disabled, ineligible, minimum-not-met, and exhausted coupons.
  10. Illustrative data: All coupon codes and accounts in these cases (for example SAVE20, customer-A, and restaurant R1) are test fixtures invented for this test suite, not actual Foodpanda codes, APIs, or implementation details.
